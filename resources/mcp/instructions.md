This server runs :app's email marketing on its own server: its lists, its subscribers, its email templates and campaign emails, and the campaigns its queue sends at a set pace.

- Call get_overview first: what is set up, what is switched off, the lists in use with their counts, the campaigns running, and the placeholders an email may use.
- Read the guide (the campaigns://guide resource) before writing or sending a campaign.
- The order is: write the email, upload it, open its preview, send a test, preview the audience, run the pre-send checks, then ask the person you work for to approve the exact recipient count and pace. Only then start.
- Never start, resume or speed up a campaign without that approval. Pausing and cancelling never wait.
- Starting answers at once: read the campaign's status until it is sending, scheduled, or a draft with a start error.
- Unsubscribes, spam reports and hard bounces stop an address for good. Never add such a person back.
- A refused call says why. Tell the person what it says rather than retrying the same call.
